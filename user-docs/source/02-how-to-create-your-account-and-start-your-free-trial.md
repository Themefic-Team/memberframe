# How to Create Your Account and Start Your Free Trial

Your MemberFrame account connects the plugin to a site where your members, tiers, and settings are stored. This page shows you how to create the account, start your 7-day free trial, and find your account details later.

## Step 1: Open the account screen

Click the account icon at the top right of the plugin. You can also click **Start free trial** in the yellow **Try MemberFrame free for 7 days** banner on the **Home** tab, which shows while the plugin is signed out.

The **Profile** screen will open with two buttons, **Sign Up** and **Sign In**.

[Add Profile Signed Out Screenshot]

## Step 2: Sign up or sign in in your browser

Click **Sign Up** if you are new, or **Sign In** if you already have a MemberFrame account. A window will open with a short line about what happens next: continuing creates your account, which activates your trial and manages your license, and a **Privacy** link shows how your details are used.

Click **Start 7-Day Free Trial**, or **Sign In with Browser** if you are signing in. A new browser tab will open with the MemberFrame sign-in page, because signing in in your browser keeps your password out of the Framer window.

In the browser tab, you can sign up with your name, email address, and password, then type the confirmation code that is emailed to you. You can also sign in with Google, or sign in with a code sent to your email. When you are done, the tab will tell you to return to Framer.

[Add Browser Sign Up Screenshot]

While you sign in, the plugin shows **Waiting for browser completion...**. It will connect by itself when you finish, so you do not need to copy anything back.

> If the plugin says the window timed out, click **Re-open Browser Window** to open the sign-in tab again.

## Step 3: Start exploring

When you come back to Framer, your free trial will start and a **You're all set!** window will show what is included. Click **Start Exploring** to begin, and the **Get started with MemberFrame** guide will open with the seven setup steps. You can read about the guide in [Getting Started with MemberFrame](01-getting-started.md).

[Add Trial Started Screenshot]

A bar at the top of the plugin will count down the days left in your trial, and it switches to hours, minutes, and seconds on the last day. Click **Upgrade** in that bar at any time to choose a plan.

## Step 4: Check your account details

Click the account icon again to see your **Profile**. It shows your name and email under **Personal Info**, your current plan under **Plan Type**, and a **Manage Subscription** button where you can choose or change your MemberFrame plan.

[Add Profile Signed In Screenshot]

Below **Personal Info** is **Site Domains**, where you list the domains your site is published on. Your live site only works once its domain is there, as explained in [How to add your site's domain](03-how-to-add-your-site-domain.md).

Click **Sign Out** at the bottom of the **Profile** screen when you want to sign the plugin out. Your site, members, and settings are kept, and they come back when you sign in again.

## Signing in on another computer or project

MemberFrame keeps your sign in in the browser you used, for the project you used it in. This keeps your account safe when you share a Framer project with someone else.

- **Another computer or browser** opens the plugin signed out, so sign in once more and your site will come back with all its members and settings.
- **A teammate** who edits the same project sees the plugin signed out, and signs in with their own MemberFrame account.
- **A duplicated or remixed project** opens signed out too, so the copy never uses the original project's account by accident.

If you are already signed in but the open project is not linked to a site yet, **Profile** shows a **Link This Project** button under the same line about your account and privacy. Click it to link the project to your account, which may start your free trial. Nothing is sent to MemberFrame until you click it.

## Good to know

- Each Framer project you connect is its own site, with its own members, tiers, and settings.
- The free trial lasts 7 days and needs no card. You get one trial per MemberFrame account, email address, and site, so a second project on the same account shows **Trial already claimed** and asks you to upgrade.
- Screens that need a connected project show a **Connect this project first** message until you sign in.

## Check that it works

Open the **Manage** tab. If you see the **Members**, **Tiers**, and **Payments** views instead of a message about connecting, your project is connected and ready.
