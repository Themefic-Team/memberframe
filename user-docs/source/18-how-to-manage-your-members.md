# How to Manage Your Members

The **Members** view lists everyone with an account on your site, with their tier and status. Use it to find a member, help them sign in, move them to another tier, or remove their access.

## Video tutorial

[Add Manage Members Video Tutorial]

## Step 1: Find a member

Click the **Manage** tab, then **Members**. Type a name or email into **Search name or email**, or narrow the list with the tier and status filters next to it.

[Add Members List Screenshot]

Each row shows the member's name, email, tier, the date they joined, and how much they have spent. A small mark after the name shows which provider they signed up with, such as Google, and the badge on the right shows their status.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | The member's access is working normally. |
| **In trial** | The member is in a trial period. |
| **Payment failed** | A renewal payment failed and is being retried. The member keeps access during your grace period. |
| **Canceled** | The member's access was revoked or their plan was cancelled. |

If no member matches your filters, click **Clear filters** to see everyone again.

## Step 2: Open a member's details

Click a member to open their details in a popup. The top shows their name, email, tier, and status.

[Add Member Details Popup Screenshot]

The popup also shows these details.

- **Sign-in methods:** how they signed up, every provider linked to their account with when it was last used, and whether they have a password.
- **Profile:** their answers to your profile questions.
- **Purchases:** their payments and subscriptions.

## Step 3: Take an action

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tier** | Moves the member to another tier, for example to give them a free upgrade. |
| **Send magic link** | Emails the member a one-click sign-in link, which helps when they cannot remember how they signed up. |
| **Revoke access** | After you confirm, signs the member out everywhere, moves them to Free, and cancels their subscription at your payment gateway at once, with no refund. Their invoice history is kept. |
| **Restore access** | Gives a revoked member their access back on the Free tier. To get a paid tier back, they buy it again. |
| **Delete member** | After you confirm, removes the member, their sessions, and their bookmarks for good. They can sign up again later as a new member. |

Changes to a member apply the next time their session is checked, which happens within moments of them loading a page.

> Changing a member's tier here does not change what their payment gateway charges. Deleting a member does not cancel their subscription either, so if you want billing to stop, use **Revoke access** or cancel the subscription in your gateway's dashboard.

## Check that it works

Change a test member's tier, then sign in as that member on your published site. Pages locked to the new tier should open for them.
