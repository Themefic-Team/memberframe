# How to Read and Export Your Reports

The **Reports** tab shows how your membership is doing: who joined, who pays, how much you earn, and each member's history. Every report can be exported as a CSV file for spreadsheets or as a PDF to share.

## Video tutorial

[Add Reports Video Tutorial]

## Step 1: Choose the payments and the period

Click the **Reports** tab. Use the switch at the top to choose **Live** or **Test** payments, and pick a period: **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **Last 12 months**, or **All time**. The switch starts on your site's current payment mode, and a **Test mode** banner reminds you when you are looking at test figures.

[Add Reports Filters Screenshot]

## Step 2: Pick a report

Choose a report in the bar under the filters.

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | **Members**, **Paying members**, **MRR**, **Net revenue**, **Active subscriptions**, and **Ended**. Below them: **Members By Tier**, a **Sign Ups** chart, your **Most Saved** bookmarks, and your **Most Downloaded** files. |
| **Revenue** | **Net revenue**, **Payments**, **Refunded**, and **MRR**, then **Revenue By Month**, revenue **By Tier** and **By Gateway**, and a **Payment History** you can filter by tier. |
| **Plans** | Subscriptions that are **Active**, **Payment failed**, **Canceling**, **Started**, and **Ended**, **Paying By Tier**, and a list of **Subscriptions** you can filter by status and tier. |
| **Members** | A card for each tier. Open one to see its members, then open a member to see their tier, lifetime spend, subscriptions, and payments. |

[Add Reports Overview Screenshot]

## What the numbers mean

| Number | How it is worked out |
| --- | --- |
| **Members** | Everyone with an account on your site. |
| **Paying members** | Members with a paid subscription that is active, has a failed payment being retried, or is canceling but has not ended yet. |
| **MRR** | Monthly recurring revenue: each monthly subscription's price, plus each yearly subscription's price divided by 12. Lifetime payments do not renew, so they are not part of MRR. |
| **Net revenue** | Payments received in the period, minus refunds. |
| **Canceling** | The member cancelled, but keeps access until the end of the period they paid for. |
| **Ended** | Subscriptions that stopped in the period. |

> Amounts in different currencies are never added together. If you changed your site currency, payments in the old currency show on their own under **Payments in other currencies**.

## Step 3: Export a report

Click **Export CSV** or **Export PDF** at the top of any report. The file will download with the report's name, the mode, and the period in its file name, and a message will confirm the export.

[Add Report Export Screenshot]

CSV files open in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets. Text typed by members is made safe, so it can never run as a spreadsheet formula.

## Check that it works

Make a test purchase in Test mode, then open **Reports** with the switch on **Test**. The payment should show in **Revenue**, and the new subscription in **Plans**.
