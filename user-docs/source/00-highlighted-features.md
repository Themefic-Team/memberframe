# MemberFrame Highlighted Features

These features are listed from most important to least important. Each one describes something that works in MemberFrame today, with the plan it belongs to and where to find it in the plugin so the right screenshot can be taken. Every feature is included in the 7-day free trial and in every MemberFrame plan (Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime).

## 1. Sell memberships from Framer

One-line summary: Create Free and paid tiers with monthly, yearly, and lifetime prices, all inside Framer.

Description: You build your membership plans right where you design your site, so there is no separate dashboard to learn. Each tier can have a monthly, a yearly, and a one-time lifetime price, plus a list of features that shows on your pricing table. Your Free tier is where every new member starts, so people can join before they pay.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Manage tab, then Tiers, then open a tier

## 2. Take payments with Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, or Razorpay

One-line summary: Connect one, two, or all three payment gateways and let buyers choose how to pay.

Description: Paste your keys and MemberFrame sets up the rest, including the webhook for Stripe and Lemon Squeezy. Buyers see the gateways you turn on, with the one that suits their location picked for them, and you can test everything in Test mode before you charge real money. Lemon Squeezy handles sales tax and VAT for you, and Razorpay adds UPI and net banking for buyers in India.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Manage tab, then Payments

## 3. Lock pages for members only

One-line summary: Make any page, or every page under a path, open only to the members you choose.

Description: Pick a page, choose who can open it, and choose where everyone else is sent, such as your sign in page or your pricing page. A path also locks every page under it, so one lock can protect a whole course or every post in a blog. Locks change on your live site the moment you save them, without publishing again.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Settings tab, then Page locks

## 4. Ready-made sign in and sign up

One-line summary: Add sign in, sign up, password reset, and email code sign in forms in one click.

Description: Members can sign in with a password, with a six-digit code sent to their email, or with Google, Apple, GitHub, or Discord. Every form can sit on the page or open as a popup, and you style it from Framer's right sidebar so it matches your brand. You also choose the password rules once, and every form on your site follows them.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Builder tab, then Member Auth or Member Login

## 5. A pricing table that sells your tiers

One-line summary: Show your tiers, prices, and features in a pricing table with checkout built in.

Description: The pricing table reads your real tiers and prices, so it always matches what you set up in the plugin. Visitors switch between monthly and yearly prices, see how much a yearly plan saves, and pay through your gateways. Members who already pay can upgrade from the same table and get credit for the time they have not used.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Builder tab, then Member Pricing

## 6. A member dashboard for self-service

One-line summary: Give members one page to see their plan, change it, pay, and update their profile.

Description: Members see their tier, their status, their next renewal, and their payment history with receipts. They can upgrade, downgrade, cancel, or resume their plan themselves, which saves you a lot of support messages. They can also fill in the profile questions you ask in Settings.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Builder tab, then Member Dashboard

## 7. Soft paywall for articles

One-line summary: Give readers a few free articles, or always a short preview, then ask them to subscribe.

Description: Place the Soft Paywall right below any article, blog post, or list and it locks that content, with no code to write. Choose a free articles meter, such as three articles every 30 days, or a read more teaser that always shows the opening paragraphs. Google still sees the full article and is told it is behind a paywall, so your search ranking is safe.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Builder tab, then Soft Paywall

## 8. Hide or blur parts of a page

One-line summary: Show sections only to members, or blur them for everyone else, on any page.

Description: Create a section lock in the plugin, then pick it on any layer in Framer. Visitors without access either do not see the section at all or see it blurred and not clickable. This is a simple way to tease members-only content on an otherwise open page.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Settings tab, then Page locks, then Section Locks

## 9. Protected file downloads

One-line summary: Share files only with the right members, or behind a password, with links nobody can share.

Description: Drop a file into the Protected Download button in Framer's sidebar and choose who can download it. Each download link works for only 60 seconds, so a copied link is useless to anyone else. Visitors without access see a button that leads them to sign in or to upgrade.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Builder tab, then Protected Download

## 10. Manage every member in one place

One-line summary: Search members, change their tier, send a sign-in link, or revoke access in seconds.

Description: Find any member by name or email and filter by tier or status. Open a member to see how they signed up, their profile answers, and their purchases, then move them to another tier or email them a one-click sign-in link. Revoking access also cancels their subscription at your payment gateway, so they are not charged again.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Manage tab, then Members, then open a member

## 11. Reports with CSV and PDF export

One-line summary: See members, revenue, MRR, and subscriptions, and export every report as CSV or PDF.

Description: The Reports tab shows how your membership is growing, how much you earn each month, and which tiers sell best. You can look at the last 30 days, 90 days, 12 months, or all time, and switch between live and test payments. Every report can be saved as a CSV file for spreadsheets or a PDF to share.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Reports tab

## 12. Social sign in with Google, Apple, GitHub, and Discord

One-line summary: Let members sign in with an account they already have, in one click.

Description: Each provider has a setup popup with the redirect address to copy and a link to its setup guide. A member who signs in with a second provider on the same verified email keeps one account. You can see in Manage how each member signed up.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Settings tab, then Integration, then Sign in

## 13. Custom profile questions

One-line summary: Ask members extra questions, such as their company, role, or portfolio link.

Description: Add up to 20 fields, choose from seven field types, and mark the important ones as required. A field can show only after a certain answer, so members only see questions that apply to them. Answers show on the member's dashboard, in Manage, and in your webhooks.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Settings tab, then Fields

## 14. Bookmarks for members

One-line summary: Let members save posts and pages to a personal list they can open from any device.

Description: Put a Bookmark Button on your blog cards or pages and a Bookmarks Tray in your navigation bar. Members save items with one click and find them again in a slide-out drawer. Saved items follow the member across devices because they are stored on their account.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Builder tab, then Bookmark Button or Bookmarks Tray

## 15. Webhooks to your other tools

One-line summary: Send new members, profile changes, and plan changes to Zapier, Make, Zoho Flow, or your server.

Description: Choose which events to send, which tiers they apply to, and exactly which fields go out under which names. A test button sends a sample member so you can check your setup before a real member joins. Each message is signed, so your server can trust it came from MemberFrame.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Settings tab, then Integration, then Webhooks

## 16. Emails from your own address

One-line summary: Send sign-in codes and reset links from your own email address with custom SMTP.

Description: Connect your own email server so members get codes and links from your address and your site name. A test button sends one email through your server so you can see the real result. If you never set it up, MemberFrame sends these emails for you.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Settings tab, then Config, then Email

## 17. Member pages in one click

One-line summary: Create your sign in, sign up, reset, dashboard, and pricing pages with one click.

Description: Tick the pages you need and MemberFrame creates them in your project with the right component already on each one. Pages that already exist are skipped, so you never get duplicates. Each page is created at the address you chose in Settings.

Plan: Free trial and every MemberFrame plan

Where to find it in the app: Builder tab, then Predefined Pages

## Top 5 for the listing

1. **Sell memberships from Framer:** Free and paid tiers with monthly, yearly, and lifetime prices.
2. **Take payments with Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, or Razorpay:** buyers choose how to pay.
3. **Lock pages for members only:** protect any page or a whole section of your site.
4. **Ready-made sign in and sign up:** passwords, email codes, and social sign in.
5. **A member dashboard for self-service:** members manage their own plan and billing.
